# Nomenclature and methodological limits

- **PG-LSTM**: manuscript terminology, chosen to avoid unsupported attribution of predictive gains to physically motivated loss terms.
- **PI-LSTM V2.4 V1**: immutable original identifier of the trained model/checkpoints and notebooks. Renaming files would hinder traceability; do not rewrite trained model architecture.
- **Physics-guided architecture**: clear-sky representation, radiative identity GHI = DNI×max(cos(z),0) + DHI by construction, loss terms for global reconstruction and direct/diffuse partition.
- **No ablation / no multi-seed experiments**: benchmark superiority does not demonstrate a causal benefit of individual physics-guided terms.
- **TEST**: retrospectively known future meteorological features during 2023–2024; the TEST experiment is not an operational real-time forecast.
- **RAW**: recursive uncorrected 2050 resource. **POST_CANDIDATE**: monthly constrained candidate. **FINAL**: annually reconciled and accepted 2050 resource.
- **No retrospective FINAL**: 2023 and 2024 POST_CANDIDATE did not admit a common annual reconciliation factor.
- **CMMY**: historical representative year composed of months selected near NSRDB monthly climatological medians. It is **not** an official TMY. FINAL 2050 is much less variable at daily and sub-daily scales.
- **2050**: calendar/solar-geometry planning assignment, not a meteorological climate projection.
